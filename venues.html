Venue Clusters

Events will take place across multiple stunning locations in Northern Italy

Milano

Main Hub

The heart of the Games, hosting ice events and the spectacular Opening Ceremony at San Siro Stadium.

Sports:

  • Ice Hockey - PalaSharp & Santa Giulia Arena
  • Figure Skating - Milano Santa Giulia Arena
  • Short Track - Milano Santa Giulia Arena
  • Speed Skating - Milano Santa Giulia Arena
🎭 Opening Ceremony 🏟️ 4 Venues

Cortina d'Ampezzo

Mountain Cluster

The "Queen of the Dolomites" returns to the Olympic stage after hosting the 1956 Winter Games.

Sports:

  • Alpine Skiing - Tofana
  • Bobsled - Eugenio Monti Sliding Centre
  • Luge - Eugenio Monti Sliding Centre
  • Skeleton - Eugenio Monti Sliding Centre
  • Curling - Cortina Olympic Stadium
🏔️ Dolomites 🏟️ 3 Venues

Bormio

Speed Events

Famous for its challenging Stelvio run, one of the most demanding downhill courses in the world.

Sports:

  • Alpine Skiing - Men's Downhill
  • Alpine Skiing - Men's Super-G
  • Alpine Skiing - Men's Combined
⛷️ Stelvio 🏟️ 1 Venue

Livigno

Freestyle Hub

A purpose-built freestyle venue in the stunning Livigno valley, perfect for aerial sports.

Sports:

  • Freestyle Skiing - All Events
  • Snowboard - Halfpipe, Slopestyle, Big Air
  • Snowboard Cross
  • Ski Cross
🎿 Freestyle 🏟️ 2 Venues

Antholz-Anterselva

Nordic Events

World-renowned biathlon venue that has hosted numerous World Cup and World Championship events.

Sports:

  • Biathlon - All Events
🎯 Biathlon 🏟️ 1 Venue

Predazzo

Jumping Events

Home to impressive ski jumping hills that have hosted FIS World Championships.

Sports:

  • Ski Jumping - All Events
  • Nordic Combined
🦅 Jumping 🏟️ 1 Venue

Tesero

Cross-Country

The picturesque Val di Fiemme will host all cross-country skiing events.

Sports:

  • Cross-Country Skiing - All Events
🎿 Nordic 🏟️ 1 Venue

Verona

Ceremony Venue

The historic Arena di Verona, a Roman amphitheatre, will host the spectacular Closing Ceremony.

Events:

  • Closing Ceremony - Arena di Verona
